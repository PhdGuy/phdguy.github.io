About me

I am a passionate researcher, an explorer of ideas, and someone who believes that knowledge has the power to create meaningful change. What excites me most is exploring the relationship between nature and society, particularly how urbanization, climate change, and human activities shape our environment and livelihoods. As a Geography researcher specializing in wetlands, urban ecology, and environmental sustainability, I find immense satisfaction in discovering new perspectives, solving complex environmental challenges, and contributing to scientific knowledge. Beyond research, I am excited by meaningful conversations, meeting inspiring people, exploring new places, learning new skills, and embracing opportunities that push me beyond my comfort zone. I believe in continuous growth, curiosity, and making a positive difference in the world.

My Research

My work focuses on the East Kolkata Wetlands, a Ramsar site where ecology, urban growth and livelihoods meet.

🌿

Wetland Ecosystem Services

Understanding the natural benefits wetlands provide, from wastewater treatment to fisheries and agriculture.

🏙️

Urbanization Impacts

Examining how a growing city reshapes wetland landscapes and puts pressure on fragile ecosystems.

👥

Livelihoods and Communities

Studying the socio-economic lives of communities who depend on wetlands for their income and food.
